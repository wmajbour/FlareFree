# FlareFree 🍃 — AI-Powered Dietary Guidance for Autoimmune Wellness

FlareFree is a mobile-first AI-powered application dedicated to empowering individuals with autoimmune conditions to live healthier lives through smarter dietary choices.

---

## ✨ Our Mission

Autoimmune diseases like rheumatoid arthritis, lupus, celiac disease, and Type 1 diabetes make daily life incredibly complex — especially when it comes to diet.

At FlareFree, we believe no one should have to navigate these challenges alone.

Our app leverages intelligent dietary recommendations, personalized based on your symptoms and dietary restrictions, to help reduce inflammation, improve energy levels, and support overall wellness — all from the palm of your hand.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Personalized Recommendations**  
  Enter your current symptoms and diet restrictions, and FlareFree suggests foods tailored to your unique condition.

- **AI-Powered Insights** *(coming soon)*  
  Future versions will integrate GPT-based AI to provide even smarter, dynamically generated nutrition advice based on the latest medical research.

- **Simple, Friendly Design**  
  Built mobile-first for a clean, accessible experience — whether you're at home, at work, or on the go.

- **Secure and Private**  
  We prioritize your health information and privacy at every step.
